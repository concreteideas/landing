The material

A material that is both structural and expressive.

Concrete gives us a balance of mass, durability and quiet visual variation. We work with a cementitious matrix and carefully selected aggregates, pigments and additives to develop the required strength, colour and surface character.

Where a finish calls for additional texture, the surface is developed through controlled forming, tooling and hand-finishing rather than relying on a perfectly uniform industrial surface.

Materials

Three material systems, selected by scale and purpose.

01

GRC — Glass Reinforced Concrete

Glass fibre reinforced concrete combines a refined concrete surface with a lightweight, high-strength matrix. It is suited to architectural forms where reduced weight and crisp detailing are important.

02

Glass & Iron Mesh

For larger pots, layered glass and iron mesh reinforcement provides additional structural integrity, helping the form maintain strength and stability as its scale increases.

03

Hardened High-Strength Concrete

A dense, high-strength concrete system used for smaller products where compact dimensions, durability and a substantial material character are the priority.

The process

From form to finished piece.

01

Form & mould

Each piece begins with a form designed around its geometry, proportions and intended finish. Mould preparation is critical to achieving clean edges and consistent surfaces.

02

Mix & cast

The concrete mix is prepared for the required application, with attention to aggregate, pigmentation, workability and reinforcement where appropriate.

03

Set & cure

After casting, the piece is allowed to develop strength through controlled curing. This stage supports dimensional stability and the performance expected from a substantial concrete object.

04

Finish & inspect

Once cured, pieces are de-moulded, finished and inspected. Edges, surfaces, colour and overall form are checked before the piece is prepared for delivery.
