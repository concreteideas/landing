# Concrete Ideas Content Guide

**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines how content should be written for the Concrete Ideas website.

The objective is to ensure that every page, product description, collection introduction, and call-to-action reflects the brand's premium, architectural positioning.

This guide applies to:

* Website
* Product pages
* Collection pages
* Projects
* Brochures
* Catalogues
* Social media
* Blog articles
* AI-generated content

---

# Content Principles

Every piece of content should be:

* Clear
* Calm
* Architectural
* Informative
* Honest
* Minimal
* Timeless

Avoid writing that feels overly promotional or sales-oriented.

---

# Writing Style

Write in short, well-structured paragraphs.

Use active voice.

Prefer simple language over technical jargon.

Be confident without exaggeration.

Avoid unnecessary adjectives.

Example:

✅ Good

Crafted from architectural concrete for contemporary residential and commercial spaces.

❌ Avoid

An amazingly beautiful premium planter with unmatched quality.

---

# Tone of Voice

Our tone is:

* Professional
* Sophisticated
* Warm
* Knowledgeable
* Understated

Never sound:

* Aggressive
* Promotional
* Pushy
* Casual
* Trendy

---

# Website Page Structure

Every page should answer four questions:

1. What is this?
2. Why does it matter?
3. Where can it be used?
4. What should the visitor do next?

---

# Homepage

Purpose

Introduce the brand.

Inspire visitors.

Guide them toward collections or products.

Recommended sections:

* Hero
* Brand Story
* Featured Collections
* Featured Products
* Why Concrete Ideas
* Projects
* Download Catalogue
* Contact

---

# Collection Pages

Each collection should include:

## Hero Heading

One clear headline.

Example

Landscape Collection

---

## Introduction

2–3 paragraphs explaining:

* Design intent
* Suitable environments
* Material characteristics

---

## Product Grid

Brief descriptions only.

Avoid repeating detailed specifications.

---

## CTA

Encourage visitors to:

* Explore products
* Request a quote
* Download the catalogue

---

# Product Pages

Every product page should follow the same order.

1. Hero Image
2. Product Name
3. Short Description
4. Key Features
5. Specifications
6. Available Sizes
7. Recommended Applications
8. Gallery
9. Related Products
10. Quote CTA
11. Download Catalogue

---

# Product Description Template

Each product description should contain:

## Introduction

One paragraph describing the product.

## Design Intent

Explain the inspiration or purpose.

## Material

Architectural concrete characteristics.

## Applications

Where the product is best suited.

## Closing Statement

A concise summary reinforcing the product's value.

---

# Collection Introduction Template

Every collection introduction should answer:

Why this collection exists.

Who it is designed for.

What environments it complements.

What makes it distinctive.

---

# Project Pages

Each project should include:

* Project Name
* Location
* Project Type
* Products Used
* Design Highlights
* Gallery
* Quote or Testimonial (when available)

Focus on how the products contribute to the architectural space.

---

# Call-to-Action Guidelines

Primary CTA

Request a Quote

Secondary CTA

Download Catalogue

Avoid generic CTAs such as:

* Click Here
* Learn More
* Buy Now

Preferred alternatives:

* Explore Collection
* View Product
* Discuss Your Project
* Download Catalogue

---

# SEO Guidelines

Each page should have:

Unique title

Unique meta description

Meaningful headings

Descriptive image alt text

Internal links to related products and collections

Avoid keyword stuffing.

Write naturally for people first.

---

# Product Naming

Product names should be:

* Short
* Memorable
* Timeless

Examples:

* Bhumi
* Antara
* Axis
* Terra
* Zenith

Avoid numbers or technical codes in customer-facing names.

---

# Image Captions

Keep captions informative.

Example:

Bhumi planter in an urban courtyard.

Avoid decorative or promotional captions.

---

# Specifications

Use consistent labels throughout the site:

* Material
* Finish
* Dimensions
* Weight
* Drainage
* Suitable For
* Available Sizes

Maintain identical wording across all products.

---

# Downloadable Resources

Each product may include:

* Product Catalogue
* Specification Sheet
* Installation Guide
* Care Instructions

Use consistent naming for all downloads.

---

# Accessibility

Headings should be descriptive.

Links should explain their destination.

Alt text should describe the image content, not repeat the filename.

Avoid using images to communicate essential information.

---

# AI Content Rules

When AI generates content:

* Follow this guide.
* Match the brand tone.
* Do not invent technical specifications.
* Do not exaggerate product capabilities.
* Prefer clarity over creativity.
* Maintain consistency with existing terminology.

---

# Content Review Checklist

Before publishing:

* Does the page answer the visitor's questions?
* Is the tone calm and professional?
* Is the language concise?
* Is every CTA purposeful?
* Are headings descriptive?
* Are specifications accurate?
* Are internal links included?
* Is the content aligned with the brand?

If any answer is "No", revise before publishing.

---

# Guiding Principle

Every sentence should strengthen confidence in the product and the brand.

The goal is not to persuade through marketing—it is to earn trust through clarity, craftsmanship, and thoughtful communication.
